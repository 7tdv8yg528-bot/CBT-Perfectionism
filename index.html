Chapter 1 • What Is Perfectionism?

Healthy Excellence vs. Perfectionism & Hewitt/Flett's 3 Traits

Pages 5–12
1. Self-Oriented Perfectionism

Self-imposed expectation to be flawless. Unrealistically high standards followed by harsh self-criticism and rumination over mistakes.

2. Other-Oriented Perfectionism

Demanding perfection from partners, children, or coworkers; easily finding fault, blaming, or feeling frustrated when others fall short.

3. Socially Prescribed Perfectionism

Believing that others (parents, bosses, culture) hold impossibly high expectations of you that must be met to earn worth and approval.
